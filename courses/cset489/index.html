Learning outcomes

In plain language, this course develops the following abilities.

  1. Explain main SEO techniques for business websites.
  2. Analyse keyword research, optimised content, indexing and campaign outcomes.
  3. Use online tools and resources to implement SEO campaigns.

Syllabus overview

Technical SEO

Crawlers, metadata, site architecture, canonicalisation, CDN, SSL, performance, sitemaps and tracking.

Keywords and content

Keyword tools, competitor keywords, content planning, internal and outbound links and schema markup.

Video and campaign monitoring

YouTube titles, descriptions and thumbnails, plus monitoring with Search Console and Analytics.
